Project Detail

Cross-Cultural Art Events & Dialogues

We organize artistic events that facilitate creative clashes and dialogue between artists of different cultures.

Through shared exhibitions, collaborative workshops, and joint artist showcases, we ask: How do artists from different traditions interpret and express the same human theme differently? And how do they express it similarly?

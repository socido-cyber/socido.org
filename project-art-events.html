Future Initiative

Cross-Cultural Art Events

Art will give us a shared language to explore universal human experiences. We will bring together multicultural artists to collaborate, create, and showcase works centered on common themes—such as harvest, family, reunion, and storytelling.

By examining how different traditions interpret the same core ideas, these upcoming events will spark meaningful dialogue and reveal how deeply connected we truly are.

Universal Themes We Will Explore

🌾

Harvest

Gratitude and seasonal celebrations to be explored across traditions.

🏡

Family & Reunion

Honoring ancestry, home, and coming together in future exhibits.

📜

Storytelling

Folklore, oral histories, and artistic mythologies brought to life.

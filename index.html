Building Cultural Bridges

Understanding That Deep Down,
We Are More Similar Than Different

"In a world of increasing tension and segregation, we believe that more interaction and more knowledge of each other is the only way to ease the tension."

Our local community is diverse and consists of many first-generation immigrants. As fellow parents, we want our children growing up to be familiar with their own cultures, and appreciative of other cultures in how they are different from their own, and most importantly, understand that deep down we are all more similar than different.

Our Initiatives

Current & Future Projects

How we kindle curiosity, artistic expression, and genuine cross-cultural connection in youth.

Language + X Programs

Organizing free language classes combined with creative arts (e.g., Language + Theatre, Language + Dance) to kindle interest in children and teens for a new language and culture.

Cross-Cultural Art Events

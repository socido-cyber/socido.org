Our Core Belief

"In a world of increasing tension and segregation, more interaction and knowledge of each other is the only way to ease the tension."

As fellow parents in a diverse community of first-generation immigrants, we want our children growing up familiar with their own cultures while appreciating others—learning that beneath our unique heritage, we share the same human values.

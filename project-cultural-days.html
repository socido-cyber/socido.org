Project Detail

School & Community Cultural Days

We partner with local schools to elevate their annual Cultural Day events, transforming them into rich, community-wide celebrations.

Elevated culture day events: brings in the volunteers, brings together the communities, frees the teachers, and brings out the fun!

By bringing in diverse performance groups, hands-on cultural workshops, and dedicated volunteers, we help school communities celebrate their diversity while taking the heavy operational lifting off teachers' shoulders.
